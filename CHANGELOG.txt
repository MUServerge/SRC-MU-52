  moved/animated the facing of those borderline triangles flipped frame to frame and they were culled
  in and out - that flicker. Fix: draw the shadow double-sided (DisableCullFace before the pass,
  EnableCullFace to restore the scene default afterwards). Verified in-game with a temporary
  GL_DEPTH_BITS readout (driver already gives 24-bit depth), which ruled out the depth-precision
  theory; that diagnostic and an experimental glPolygonOffset/pixel-format change were reverted so
  this fix is just the cull-face change.
